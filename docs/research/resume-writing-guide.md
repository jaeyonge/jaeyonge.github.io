# Resume writing sources and reusable rules

These are English official career resources, not universal hiring policies. Apply a job posting’s explicit requirements first. Review links when updating their advice; do not invent a publication date or treat a search snippet as a complete document.

- [Amazon: resume tips](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/workplace/amazon-job-application-resume-writing-tips): select evidence that matches qualifications, show achievements and supported scale, keep layout simple.
- [Microsoft Tech Transition Toolkit](https://careers.microsoft.com/content/dam/microsoftcareersvnext/en/pdfs/Microsoft_Tech_Transition_Toolkit.pdf): concise role-relevant evidence and careful editing; its transition audience is specific.
- [Google: changing careers](https://grow.google/grow-your-career/articles/career-change/): show transferable capabilities through examples. This is career education, not a Google hiring rule.
- [Google Careers application help](https://support.google.com/googlecareers/answer/6095391?hl=en): check parsed fields and submission requirements; do not assume parsing is perfect.
- [Harvard: creating a strong resume](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/): specific active factual writing, relevant order, proofreading and truthful use of AI assistance.
- [MIT: writing about skills](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes-writing-about-your-skills/): connect project, action and result; scope can matter even without an outcome metric.
- [MIT: resume checklist](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resume-checklist/): verify dates, degrees, titles and consistent formatting.
- [MIT: undergraduate resume guide](https://capd.mit.edu/blog/2023/09/01/enhance-your-resume-a-guide-for-first-year-undergraduates/): readable chronological structure and awareness that complex visual layouts can affect parsing. Audience is undergraduates.
- [MIT: data science projects](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/highlighting-your-data-science-project/): show problem, data, preparation, method, validation and limits.
- [MIT: 2026 resume resources](https://capd.mit.edu/blog/2026/06/23/interphase-2026-resume-resources/): follow requested format and inspect plain-text extraction rather than claim universal ATS guarantees.

Repository choices: three bullets per company/research are the owner’s editorial rule, not a rule all sources impose. A personal website may carry decorative logos; the generated submission PDF uses a simple text-focused print layout. Never claim that any format guarantees ATS success. Keep original research notes and personal evidence private in `sources/`.
